# TASK 16197 — literal brief (investigate with a reproduction; do not guess)

Issue: https://github.com/tangency-labs/solidcraft/issues/16197

## The report (in French, translated)

Title: "How to prevent the colouring of a closed zone in a sketch?"

"What happened: when a set of lines defines the edge of a closed zone, it gets a pale
orange colour. That is handy if you want to build an extrusion from that sketch
later; it should be possible to enable that by clicking on a button somewhere.

But if you click INSIDE a closed zone that is in ANOTHER sketch, while you are in
sketch mode, that zone gets coloured DARK BLUE, and you can no longer see it.

This part of the interface needs to be reworked completely."

Steps to reproduce: "click in a closed zone, when you are in sketch mode".

Version: 0.40.1, Windows 10.

There is no screenshot and no recording in the report, and no comment on the issue.

## What the report means, in terms of the product

* A sketch region whose edges form a closed loop is highlighted (pale orange) when
  it is a profile that can be extruded.
* Clicking inside such a closed zone WHILE IN SKETCH MODE, when that zone belongs to
  ANOTHER sketch (a different document/tab), paints the zone DARK BLUE and the zone
  becomes unreadable. It is a selection/highlight bug in the interaction between the
  pick of a sketch-mode viewport and the sketch profiles of the OTHER open designs.

So the defect is in the UI layer: the pick in sketch mode must not select or
repaint the profiles of a different design.
