## What the issue is

Extruding the band between an ellipse and its offset gives a wall made of ~250 faces instead of
an inner wall, an outer wall and two caps. The offset of an ellipse is stored as a spline with
257 points, and the profile loop keeps one piece per point, so the kernel gets a loop of ~500
edges. A Chamfer 1 mm on one of those edges is refused ("1 mm is too large for this chamfer: on a
concave edge it would shrink the part by 1562.29 mm³ instead"), after about 11 s. The same
chamfer on the equivalent circle band works in 0.3 s and gives 5 faces.

The reporter's own read: "I think this happens in the sketch/profile step (one piece per segment
of the offset spline), not in the kernel, so I am reporting it as a bug rather than under
`kernel-perf`. A circle does not show it."

Expected (from the issue): "a band between an ellipse and its offset extrudes to a few faces
(inner wall, outer wall, two caps), as it does for a circle, and a chamfer on its top edge is
either done or refused quickly."
