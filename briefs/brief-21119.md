## The cause, on the current `main` (file:line)

**`crates/sketch/src/redundancy.rs:109` — `fn classify_rows`** is where the dimension is called
redundant, reached from **`crates/sketch/src/redundancy.rs:215` — `pub fn determined_by`**, which
the engine calls at **`crates/engine/src/cmd/sketch.rs:3352`** and
**`crates/engine/src/cmd/sketch.rs:3464`**.

Why:

1. An **arc** has no radius variable. `Layout::rvar` only covers `CurveKind::Circle` and
   `CurveKind::Ellipse` (`crates/sketch/src/solver.rs:226-239`), so an arc's radius is derived:
   `r = |centre − end|`.
2. The arc's own block row (the relation that keeps the two ends at the same radius from the
   centre) is, at the exact half circle, purely in x: the centre is on the perpendicular
   bisector of the chord, so the only remaining freedom is the centre's y (call it `h`).
3. `r = sqrt((c/2)^2 + h^2)`, so `dr/dh = h/r = 0` at `h = 0`. The radius dimension's row
   projects to **zero** against the directions the earlier rows already span, so
   `classify_rows` finds that the dimension adds no rank and calls it dependent (redundant).
4. The same zero row is why the solver refuses `value: "12"`: from `h = 0` the radius is at its
   minimum and there is no first-order direction to grow it.
5. The degrees of freedom are `lay.n - rank` (`crates/sketch/src/solver.rs:4271`), and the rank
   comes from the Jacobian rank analysis around `crates/sketch/src/solver.rs:4118-4121`, so the
   singular row also keeps the dof at 1.

The maintainer (WilliamHJKL) traced this in the issue and proposed the shape of the fix:

- Before solving, move the free centre of an arc a little off its chord when a driving radius or
  diameter is on the arc, its centre lies on the chord, and the target differs from the current
  radius. This is the same idea as `nudge_line_angles`
  (`crates/sketch/src/solver.rs:2938`, called at `crates/sketch/src/solver.rs:3837`), which
  nudges a wrapped angle off its discontinuity before Newton.
- Test redundancy on a slightly moved copy of the variables as well, so a constraint is called
  redundant only when it is dependent at **both** points. Two vertical constraints on one line
  stay redundant everywhere; this radius is only dependent at `h = 0`.

There is already a "step a short way along the freedoms and re-analyse" mechanism in the solver
(`Nearby::determined`, `crates/sketch/src/solver.rs:2200-2260`), but it only re-evaluates **which**
unknowns are determined and requires the rank to stay the same, so it does not cover this case.
Read it before you decide.

## The two traps of this fix (bold on purpose)

- **The nudge must NOT move the solved geometry.** The nudge is only a starting point for the
  solver. After the fix, the half circle must still measure the same: the line still measures
  `20.0` mm, the arc's radius still measures `10.0` mm, the arc's ends are still on the line's
  ends, and the arc's centre is still on the chord. If the fix leaves the arc visibly bent,
  flattened or shifted, it is wrong.
- **A genuinely redundant constraint must STILL be reported as redundant.** The existing
  redundancy tests must stay green: `crates/engine/src/cmd/sketch_redundant_tests.rs` and
  `crates/sketch/src/redundancy_tests.rs`. In particular the two-vertical-constraints-on-one-line
  case must keep reporting `redundant: true` with the dof unchanged. A fix that simply stops
  calling dimensions redundant is not a fix.
