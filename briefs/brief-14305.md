## What the issue is

A Circular Pattern that holds more than one feature fails. Adding features to the pattern gives
"there is no body to cut or intersect", and a pattern whose features include one that cuts another
gives "nothing lies between the profile and the To object".

Expected (from the issue): "All selected features will be included in the circular pattern."

The upstream triage (dudedagger42) already reproduced this on 0.40.0-rc.3 and on a main build with
the CLI, and narrowed it down:

- `timeline.redefine` of CircularPattern1 (feature 13) with features
  `["Revolve1","Revolve2","Extrude3","Chamfer1"]` fails with "there is no body to cut or intersect".
- `["Revolve1","Revolve2"]` fails the same way.
- `["Extrude3","Chamfer1"]` fails with "nothing lies between the profile and the To object".
- `["Revolve1"]` alone works.

The design's timeline (from the file): 3 Sketch1, 4 Extrude1, 5 Extrude2, 14 Sketch5, 7 Revolve1,
8 Sketch3, 9 Revolve2, 10 Sketch4, 11 Extrude3, 12 Chamfer1, 13 pattern CircularPattern1.
CircularPattern1 currently holds feature 7 (Revolve1) only.

The triage also located the message: it comes from `empty_boolean_targets`
(`crates/doc/src/eval.rs`, around line 3249). Why the pattern's cut finds no body was not traced.

Boundary the triage found: reproduced with the CLI; the app's own pattern dialog was not tried.
